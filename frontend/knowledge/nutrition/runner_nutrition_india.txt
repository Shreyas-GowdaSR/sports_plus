RUNNER NUTRITION GUIDE — INDIA

PRE-RUN (1-2 hours before): Idli with sambar — easily digestible, moderate carbs. Banana (2 medium) — quick carbohydrate, potassium for cramp prevention. Poha with peanuts — light, quick-digesting carb with protein. Ragi dosa — complex carb, iron-rich, ideal for longer runs.

POST-RUN RECOVERY (within 30 min, target 25-35g protein): Paneer bhurji + rice — 18g protein per 100g paneer, complete recovery. Dal + rice + curd — complementary proteins, complete amino acid profile. Soya chunks + chapati — 25g protein per 50g dry, most protein-dense plant source. Banana + curd + peanut butter shake — quick and easy.

CARBOHYDRATE RECOVERY: White rice — high GI, rapid glycogen replenishment. Chapati — whole wheat for sustained energy. Sweet potato — anti-inflammatory, complex carbs.

INDIAN SUPERFOODS FOR RUNNERS: Turmeric (haldi) + black pepper — curcumin reduces inflammation, must combine with pepper for 2000% better absorption. Ashwagandha — VO2max improvement, cortisol reduction, 300-600mg daily. Amla — highest natural vitamin C, essential for collagen synthesis. Beetroot juice 250ml — natural nitrates improve endurance 3-5%. Moringa leaves — iron, calcium, protein.

IRON FOR RUNNERS: Spinach, methi, rajma, horse gram, jaggery. Always combine with vitamin C to enhance absorption. Avoid tea or coffee within 1 hour of iron-rich meals — tannins reduce absorption 60%.

HYDRATION IN INDIA: Coconut water — natural electrolytes (potassium, sodium, magnesium). Buttermilk (chaas) — probiotics, sodium, potassium. Jaljeera — cooling, digestive, electrolytes. Lemon water + pink salt — natural ORS. Traditional ORS: 1L water + 6 tsp sugar + 0.5 tsp salt + lemon.

BUDGET RECOVERY MEAL: Banana + curd + peanut butter + oats — 25g protein, 60g carbs, under Rs 50.
