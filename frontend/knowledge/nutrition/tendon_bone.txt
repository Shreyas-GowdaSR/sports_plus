TENDON AND BONE HEALTH — INDIA

COLLAGEN FOR VEGETARIANS: Body synthesises collagen from glycine, proline, hydroxyproline + vitamin C. No animal collagen needed. Protocol: Amla juice (250ml, natural vitamin C) + glycine supplement (5g), 30 min before morning run. Traditional: amla powder in warm water with jaggery.

BONE HEALTH INDIA: Sesame seeds (til) — 975mg calcium per 100g, highest plant source. Add to chutney, roti, ladoo. Ragi (finger millet) — 344mg calcium per 100g. Make ragi mudde or dosa. Dairy: curd + paneer + milk. Vitamin D: supplement 2000 IU D3, especially monsoon and winter. Sunlight: 15-20 min daily before 10am.

ANTI-INFLAMMATORY INDIAN TRADITION: Turmeric haldi milk with black pepper daily — block inflammatory pathways. Fresh ginger (adrak) — gingerols reduce prostaglandins. Boswellia (shallaki) — traditional joint support. Flaxseeds (alsi) — plant-based omega-3 ALA.

AYURVEDIC RECOVERY FOODS: Moong dal — easily digestible, high lysine for tendon repair. Sattu (roasted chana flour) — high protein, iron, cooling for summer. Dates (khajoor) — natural sugar, iron, magnesium for pre-run energy. Banana + milk — potassium + protein, simple cramp prevention.

ASHWAGANDHA PROTOCOL: 300-600mg daily (KSM-66 extract preferred). Reduces exercise-induced cortisol. Improves VO2max in clinical trials. Take with warm milk or water. Best taken at night for sleep-recovery benefits.
