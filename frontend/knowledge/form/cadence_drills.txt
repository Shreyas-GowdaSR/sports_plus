CADENCE IMPROVEMENT DRILLS

A-SKIP: 4x20m. Drive knee to hip height, push off ball of foot. Cue: stamp the ground, spring up. Develops leg turnover, power, coordination. Classic athletics drill.

HIGH KNEE MARCH: 3x30m. Drive knees to hip height, arms pumping. Cue: pull knee with hip flexor. Develops hip drive and leg turnover pattern.

FAST FEET: 3x15s in place. Quick light steps 190-200 spm. Cue: pretend ground is hot. Trains neuromuscular quickness for higher cadence.

METRONOME RUN: 10 min easy at 175 BPM. Match footfalls to beat. Most effective habit-formation method. Use free metronome app.

STRIDES: 6x100m at 95% effort, full recovery. Cadence rises naturally at speed. Teaches body to operate at higher turnover without conscious effort.

ANKLE HOPS: 3x20 reps. Stiff ankle, minimal knee bend, spring rapidly. Reduces ground contact time. Critical for cadence above 175 spm.

POGO JUMPS: 3x15 both feet. Spring from ankles, land immediately. Develops reactive strength and tendon stiffness for faster cadence.
