SYMMETRY AND BALANCE DRILLS

SINGLE-LEG BALANCE: 3x30s each leg, eyes open then closed. Identifies weaker side. Progress to BOSU board. Daily habit.

SINGLE-LEG DEADLIFT: 3x10 each side. Hip hinge on one leg, back flat, reach opposite hand to floor. Strengthens glutes and hip stabilisers directly for running.

CLAMSHELLS WITH BAND: 3x15 each side. Band above knees, rotate top knee, control return. Targets gluteus medius — critical for hip-drop correction.

HIP HIKE: Stand on step, drop and raise hip on hanging leg. 3x15 each side. Directly trains hip abductors. Corrects Trendelenburg gait.

LATERAL BAND WALKS: 3 sets x10 steps each direction. Activates hip abductors in movement. Keep band taut, steps controlled.

BULGARIAN SPLIT SQUAT: 3x8 each leg, rear foot elevated. Precisely identifies and corrects leg strength asymmetry.

STEP-UP WITH KNEE DRIVE: 3x10 each leg on box. Mimics running stride, trains single-leg power symmetry.

CONTROLLED STEP-DOWN: 3x6 each side off step. Trains eccentric hip control. Keep knee tracking over middle toe.
