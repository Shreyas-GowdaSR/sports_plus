INJURY PREVENTION FOR RUNNERS

IT BAND SYNDROME: Lateral knee pain from hip weakness and crossover gait. Prevention: gluteus medius strengthening, clamshells, lateral band walks, reduce crossover.

ACHILLES TENDINOPATHY: From rapid speed or hill increase. Prevention: eccentric calf raises, gradual load progression, fish collagen or bovine collagen + vitamin C.

PLANTAR FASCIITIS: Heel pain from tight calves and weak foot intrinsics. Prevention: calf stretching, towel scrunches, arch support, reduce mileage spikes.

SHIN SPLINTS: Medial tibial stress from too much too soon. Prevention: 10% weekly mileage rule, tibialis anterior strengthening, soft surfaces.

STRESS FRACTURES: From low bone density or excessive training. Prevention: calcium, vitamin D3, adequate rest, cross-training on easy days.

PATELLOFEMORAL PAIN: Weak VMO and glutes. Prevention: terminal knee extensions, single-leg squats, reduce downhill running.

THE 10% RULE: Never increase weekly mileage more than 10% per week. Single most evidence-based injury prevention strategy in distance running.

LOAD MONITORING: If injury risk is High, reduce training load 40%. If Moderate, reduce intensity 20% and complete drill plan before adding mileage.
