RUNNING GAIT BIOMECHANICS

CADENCE: Optimal 170-180 spm. Below 160 spm causes overstriding and 20-30% higher ground reaction forces. Increase by 5-10 spm every 2 weeks. Use 175 BPM metronome. Elite: 180-200 spm.

GROUND CONTACT TIME: Elite 0.15-0.20s. Recreational target <0.25s. High GCT means poor ankle stiffness and reactive strength. Improve with ankle hops and pogo jumps.

STRIDE LENGTH: Optimal 0.9-1.2x hip height. Overstriding increases braking force 30%. Never force stride length — it increases naturally with speed and cadence.

FOOT STRIKE: Heel strike increases impact. Midfoot is balanced. Forefoot suits faster paces but increases Achilles demand. Transition gradually.

SYMMETRY: >95% = balanced. <90% = compensation risk from hip weakness or prior injury. Assess with left-right ankle excursion comparison.

TORSO LEAN: Optimal 5-10 degrees forward from ankles. >15 degrees overloads lumbar spine. Lean from ankles not waist. Core must stay engaged.

ARM SWING: Optimal elbow 85-100 degrees. Forward-backward swing, not across midline. Tight shoulders waste energy and disrupt cadence.

RUNNING ECONOMY: Improved by higher cadence, reduced GCT, optimal stride. Train with strides, tempo runs, strength work.
